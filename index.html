A free momentum scanner built Ross Cameron's way: he scans the entire market for stocks up ≥4% on the day, sorts by Change % descending, and only seriously looks at the top 5–10 rows — "page one," never page two. The formal 5 Pillars (relative volume ≥5x, up ≥10% on the day, a real news catalyst, price $1–$20 with a $5–$10 sweet spot, float under 20M/10M preferred) are still what scores the Pillars badge below. Alpha Vantage's free endpoint only gives price, % change and volume — so Price and % gain are auto-checked; Volume is real, but relative volume needs an average-volume baseline you enter once per ticker (enter it in the Rel Vol column and it's saved — without it, a rough 500k-share liquidity proxy is used instead). News freshness is checked for real per ticker — click "Check news" to pull the latest headline's actual publish time from Alpha Vantage and see it rendered as an age icon (🔥 under 2h, 🟢 under 4h, 🟡 under 12h, 🟠 under 24h, ⚪ 24h+); that result is cached per ticker per day, and until checked, a manual catalyst-type field is the fallback. That same field also tags catalyst quality — earnings/FDA/clinical/contract catalysts count toward the News pillar, but a merger/buyout/acquisition catalyst is flagged and deliberately does not count, because once a buyout price is set the stock tends to go flat. Float is filled in by hand; once entered, Float Rotation (today's volume ÷ float) is computed for you. Rows are numbered by rank and the top 3 get a leader highlight, top 10 a subtle tint, mirroring "page one of a search." A real, paid scanner (like Warrior Trading's) would also show 5-min relative volume and Short Interest — Alpha Vantage's free endpoints have neither, so they're intentionally left out here rather than faked. Data is end-of-run/delayed, not tick-by-tick real-time — treat this as a watchlist builder, not a live execution feed.

What makes a catalyst worth trading

This system's real trading window is roughly 7:00–11:00am EST, most active in the first 1–2 hours after the market opens — that's when a fresh catalyst turns into a real move. A stock with no catalyst at all can still run on a pure technical breakout or short squeeze — trade those with extra caution.

Earnings surprises
FDA approvals
Positive clinical trial results
Major new contracts or partnerships

Mergers, acquisitions, buyouts — once a buyout price is set, the stock's value is fixed and it tends to go flat, losing the volatility that makes it tradeable.
