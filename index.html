Risk rules calculator

Their first week of a small-account challenge used fixed dollar rules — risk $50 to make $100, a daily max loss of -$100, and three consecutive losing trades ends the trading day. The percentages below scale that same idea to any account size — not a mandate, just their worked example.

A momentum scanner built Ross Cameron's way: scans for stocks up ≥4% on the day, sorted by Change % descending, highlighting the top 5–10 rows — "page one," never page two.

  • 5 Pillars scoring — relative volume ≥5x, up ≥10% on the day, a real news catalyst, price $1–$20 ($5–$10 sweet spot), float under 20M (10M preferred). This is what scores the Pillars badge below.
  • Two data sources, chosen automatically — if a Financial Modeling Prep key is configured on the server (see docs/scanner-upgrade-plan.md), relative volume and float are computed for real; otherwise it falls back to the free Alpha Vantage flow, where those two need a value entered once per ticker (a rough 500k-share liquidity proxy is used until then). A manual entry always overrides the automatic value.
  • News freshness — click "Check news" to pull the latest real headline's publish time (Finnhub if configured, Alpha Vantage otherwise), shown as an age icon: 🔥 <2h · 🟢 <4h · 🟡 <12h · 🟠 <24h · ⚪ 24h+. Cached per ticker per day; a manual catalyst-type field is the fallback until checked.
  • Catalyst quality — earnings, FDA approvals, clinical results, and new contracts count toward the News pillar. A merger/buyout/acquisition catalyst is flagged and deliberately excluded — once a buyout price is set, the stock tends to go flat.
  • Ranking — rows are numbered by rank; the top 3 get a leader highlight, the top 10 a subtle tint, mirroring "page one of a search."

Data still isn't tick-by-tick real-time on either path — treat this as a watchlist builder, not a live execution feed.

What makes a catalyst worth trading

This system's real trading window is roughly 7:00–11:00am EST, most active in the first 1–2 hours after the market opens — that's when a fresh catalyst turns into a real move. A stock with no catalyst at all can still run on a pure technical breakout or short squeeze — trade those with extra caution.

Earnings surprises
FDA approvals
Positive clinical trial results
Major new contracts or partnerships

Mergers, acquisitions, buyouts — once a buyout price is set, the stock's value is fixed and it tends to go flat, losing the volatility that makes it tradeable.

Data source

Refresh always tries the upgraded scanner first (server-side Financial Modeling Prep + Finnhub — no key needed here, it's configured on Vercel) and falls back automatically to Alpha Vantage below if that's not set up yet. For the free fallback: get a key at alphavantage.co (instant, no card) — it's stored only in this browser. Free keys are rate-limited (historically ~25 requests/day), so this scanner caches results and only refreshes when you click Refresh.
